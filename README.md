## Hello

I’m a product manager who likes to tinker, prototype, and build. I’m especially interested in products that help people make data-informed decisions or reveal patterns that would otherwise be hard to see. A lot of my work ends up orbiting ranking systems, recommendation logic, and decision-support tools.

I learned Python during COVID and built my first major app, Open Water Insights, from scratch as a way to give back to the sport of open water swimming. The ranking methods I developed there were later adopted by World Aquatics.

More recently, I’ve been using AI-assisted workflows to prototype faster and explore product ideas more ambitiously. That’s been a big part of building PowderScout, a side business focused on helping skiers rank destination options under uncertainty.

This GitHub is where I experiment, build, and learn in public.
